# Code of conduct

We want SSH Manager's community to be welcoming, constructive and safe for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experiences; explain the reasoning behind feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent and accessibility needs.
- Credit others' work and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks, deliberate disruption and disclosure of someone else's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces, and to conduct while representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban repeated/severe offenders. Responses should be proportionate, documented privately and applied consistently; an immediate restriction may be appropriate to protect someone from harm.

For a private conduct concern, contact **[security@stark-ai.de](mailto:security@stark-ai.de)** with the subject `SSH Manager conduct concern` and request routing to a maintainer who is not involved. Share only the minimum necessary context. This reuses the organization's published private contact; it does not promise independent moderation or confidentiality beyond what is needed to investigate and protect participants. Do not publish personal details in an issue.

Someone named in a complaint should not decide its outcome. You may request reconsideration by an uninvolved maintainer through the same channel; if no independent reviewer is available, maintainers should say so rather than promise one. There is no guaranteed response time.
